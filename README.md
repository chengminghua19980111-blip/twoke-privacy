# Twork 隐私政策 / Twork Privacy Policy

**生效日期 / Effective date：2026 年 9 月 22 日 / September 22, 2026**

## 1. 概述

Twork 是一款在 Windows 设备上运行的人工智能工作助手，可用于对话、文件处理、任务执行以及由用户启用的网页辅助功能。

我们重视用户隐私。本政策说明 Twork 可能访问、处理、存储或传输哪些信息，以及用户如何管理这些信息。

## 2. Twork 处理的信息

根据用户使用的功能，Twork 可能处理：

- 用户输入的对话、指令和反馈；
- 用户主动选择或上传的文档、图片及其他文件；
- 项目目录、文件路径和工作区内容；
- 会话标题、消息记录、模型设置和应用偏好；
- 用户主动配置的人工智能服务提供商、模型名称和服务地址；
- 应用运行日志、错误信息和本地诊断信息；
- 使用网页辅助功能时，用户主动打开的网页内容以及填写到网页中的信息。

Twork 不会在未经用户操作或授权的情况下主动读取与当前任务无关的个人文件。

## 3. 信息的使用方式

上述信息仅用于：

- 响应用户的对话和任务请求；
- 读取、生成、修改或整理用户指定的文件；
- 保存和恢复本地会话；
- 调用用户选择的人工智能模型服务；
- 执行用户明确要求的网页浏览或表单填写操作；
- 诊断错误、维护应用稳定性和改进本地使用体验。

Twork 不出售用户个人信息，也不将用户信息用于广告画像或定向广告。

## 4. 本地存储

Twork 的会话记录、项目配置、上传文件、运行日志和偏好设置通常保存在用户自己的 Windows 设备上。

这些本地数据可能一直保留，直到用户在 Twork 中删除相应会话或文件、清理应用数据，或者卸载应用并删除其数据目录。

能够访问用户 Windows 账户或设备存储的人员，也可能访问这些本地数据。用户应使用 Windows 登录密码、磁盘加密和其他设备安全措施保护自己的设备。

## 5. 人工智能服务提供商

Twork 本身不提供基础人工智能模型。用户配置模型服务并发送消息时，相关对话、指令、附件内容或任务上下文可能被传输至用户选择的第三方人工智能服务提供商。

这些服务提供商可能包括用户自行配置的 OpenAI、DeepSeek 或其他兼容服务。第三方如何收集、保存和使用数据，由其各自的隐私政策和服务条款决定。

用户不应向第三方模型发送自己无权处理的机密信息、密码、身份凭证或其他敏感个人信息。

API 密钥或登录凭证仅用于连接用户选择的服务。Twork 不会把这些凭证用于广告或出售给第三方。

## 6. 网页浏览和表单填写

当用户主动启用网页辅助功能时，Twork 可能读取当前网页的可见内容，并根据用户指令在网页中输入信息。

Twork 不会绕过验证码、网站安全机制或用户确认。涉及最终提交、发送消息、创建申请、购买、付款或其他会产生外部影响的操作，应由用户检查并确认。

网页运营方可能按照其自身隐私政策记录用户输入的数据、IP 地址、设备信息、Cookie 和访问行为。用户在提交前应阅读相应网站的隐私政策。

## 7. 信息共享

除以下情况外，Twork 开发者不会主动向第三方共享用户信息：

- 用户要求 Twork 调用其选择的模型、网站或插件服务；
- 用户主动使用联网、网页浏览或文件上传功能；
- 为遵守适用法律、法院命令或合法政府要求；
- 为保护用户、公众或软件安全所必需。

安装或启用第三方插件后，该插件可能按照其自身功能处理数据。用户应仅安装可信来源的插件。

## 8. 遥测与广告

当前版本的 Twork 不包含第三方广告，也不使用用户对话或文件建立广告画像。

Twork 不主动运行面向开发者的云端用户行为分析服务。应用为排查故障生成的运行日志保存在本地设备上，除非用户主动将其提供给开发者或技术支持人员。

## 9. 数据安全

Twork 采用合理的技术措施限制本地服务和工作区访问，并避免在普通界面和诊断信息中显示 API 密钥等敏感凭证。

但是，任何软件、设备存储或互联网传输都无法保证绝对安全。用户应妥善保护设备、账户、API 密钥及所处理的文件。

## 10. 用户的选择与权利

用户可以：

- 不提供非必要的个人信息；
- 选择或更换人工智能服务提供商；
- 停用不需要的插件和网页辅助功能；
- 删除会话、上传文件或工作区文件；
- 清理 Twork 的本地应用数据；
- 撤销第三方服务的 API 密钥或访问权限；
- 卸载 Twork。

如需访问、更正或删除已经提交给第三方模型或网站的数据，用户还需要联系对应的第三方服务提供商。

## 11. 儿童隐私

Twork 不专门面向儿童，也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用本软件及第三方人工智能服务。

## 12. 政策更新

本政策可能随 Twork 功能、法律要求或第三方服务变化而更新。更新后的版本将发布在本页面，并修改顶部的生效日期。

## 13. 联系方式

如对本隐私政策或 Twork 的数据处理方式有疑问，可以通过以下页面联系开发者：

https://github.com/chengminghua19980111-blip/twoke-privacy/issues

# Twork Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 22, 2026**

## 1. Overview

Twork is an AI work assistant for Windows. It supports conversations, file processing, task execution, and optional browser-assisted features enabled by the user.

This policy explains what information Twork may access, process, store, or transmit and the choices available to users.

## 2. Information Processed by Twork

Depending on the features used, Twork may process:

- Conversations, instructions, and feedback entered by the user;
- Documents, images, and other files selected or uploaded by the user;
- Project folders, file paths, and workspace content;
- Session titles, message history, model settings, and application preferences;
- User-configured AI providers, model names, and service endpoints;
- Local operational logs, error details, and diagnostic information;
- Visible webpage content and information entered into webpages when browser-assisted features are used.

Twork does not intentionally access unrelated personal files without a user action or authorization.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only to:

- Respond to conversations and task requests;
- Read, create, modify, or organize user-selected files;
- Save and restore local sessions;
- Connect to an AI model service selected by the user;
- Perform browser navigation or form-filling requested by the user;
- Diagnose errors and maintain application reliability.

Twork does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

## 4. Local Storage

Conversation history, project configuration, uploaded files, operational logs, and preferences are generally stored on the user's Windows device.

Local information may remain until the user deletes the relevant content, clears the application's data, or uninstalls Twork and removes its data directory.

Anyone with access to the user's Windows account or device storage may also be able to access this information. Users should protect their devices with appropriate Windows security and disk-encryption features.

## 5. AI Service Providers

Twork does not operate the underlying AI models. When a user configures a model provider and sends a request, conversations, instructions, attachments, or task context may be transmitted to the provider selected by the user.

Providers may include OpenAI, DeepSeek, or another compatible service configured by the user. Processing by those services is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Users should not submit passwords, credentials, confidential information, or personal information they are not authorized to process.

API keys and authentication credentials are used only to connect to services selected by the user. Twork does not use them for advertising or sell them to third parties.

## 6. Browser Assistance and Form Filling

When browser-assisted features are enabled by the user, Twork may read visible webpage content and enter information according to the user's instructions.

Twork does not bypass CAPTCHA challenges, website security controls, or required user confirmation. Users should review and confirm actions that submit forms, send messages, create applications, make purchases or payments, or otherwise affect an external service.

Website operators may collect submitted information, IP addresses, device information, cookies, and browsing activity under their own privacy policies.

## 7. Disclosure of Information

The developer does not intentionally disclose user information except when:

- The user asks Twork to contact a selected model, website, or plugin service;
- The user initiates an online, browsing, or upload feature;
- Disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal request;
- Disclosure is necessary to protect users, the public, or software security.

Third-party plugins may process information according to their functionality. Users should install plugins only from sources they trust.

## 8. Analytics and Advertising

The current version of Twork contains no third-party advertising and does not use conversations or files to create advertising profiles.

Twork does not intentionally operate a cloud-based developer analytics service. Operational logs used for troubleshooting are stored locally unless the user voluntarily provides them to the developer or support personnel.

## 9. Security

Twork uses reasonable technical safeguards to limit access to local services and workspace content and to prevent sensitive credentials such as API keys from appearing in ordinary user interfaces and diagnostic output.

No software, device storage system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users remain responsible for protecting their device, accounts, API keys, and files.

## 10. User Choices and Rights

Users may:

- Decline to provide nonessential personal information;
- Select or change their AI service provider;
- Disable plugins and browser-assisted features;
- Delete conversations, uploaded files, or workspace files;
- Clear Twork's local application data;
- Revoke API keys or permissions granted to third-party services;
- Uninstall Twork.

To access, correct, or delete information already transmitted to a third-party model or website, users may also need to contact that provider directly.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Twork is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Minors should use the software and third-party AI services only with the guidance of a parent or guardian.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Twork's features, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

## 13. Contact

Questions about this policy or Twork's handling of information may be submitted at:

https://github.com/chengminghua19980111-blip/twoke-privacy/issues
